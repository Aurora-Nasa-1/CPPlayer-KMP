## 2025-02-28 - Avoid Heavy Instantiation in Frequently Recomposed Composable
**Learning:** During continuous state updates (like `state.positionMs` on playback progress), components like `Brush.radialGradient` get instantiated heavily every recomposition. Continuous instantiation of graphic objects creates enormous garbage collection and continuous CPU spikes overhead. Additionally, binding `onValueChange` directly to a seek action for a slider causes the engine to continuous update which creates massive performance drops and severe UI/engine seek stutter.
**Action:** Use `remember` block for wrapping heavy allocations (like `Brush`) when in a continuous recomposition cycle. Introduce local states like `seekValue` to manage immediate slider updates, and trigger heavy engine actions only in `onValueChangeFinished`.
